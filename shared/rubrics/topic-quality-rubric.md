# Topic Quality Rubric

| Criterion | Strong | Weak |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specificity | Names a clear task, scope, and scenario. | Broad theme or vague direction. |
| Research value | Addresses an unresolved limitation. | Only repeats existing work. |
| Feasibility | Data, methods, compute, and evaluation are realistic. | Key resources are inaccessible. |
| Evaluation | Has measurable or arguable evidence. | No clear way to validate. |
| Risk visibility | Main risks and alternatives are explicit. | Risks are hidden or ignored. |
