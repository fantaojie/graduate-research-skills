# Paper Structure Rubric

| Criterion | Strong | Weak |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Argument flow | Sections build a clear problem-method-evidence story. | Sections follow a template without logic. |
| Contribution clarity | Contributions are specific and supported. | Contributions are vague or overstated. |
| Related work role | Related work positions the gap. | Related work is a paper list. |
| Evidence mapping | Major claims point to figures, tables, or analysis. | Claims lack planned evidence. |
| Reader path | The paper is easy to follow section by section. | Readers must infer the structure. |
